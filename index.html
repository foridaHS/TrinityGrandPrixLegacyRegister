About Lavitar Racing Club

Founded on the principles of excellence and bloodline prestige, the Lavitar Racing Club is the premier destination for thoroughbred enthusiasts. Our mission is to preserve the legacy of racing while hosting the annual Legend Chronicle.

  • Elite Breeding: Access to the world's most sought-after bloodlines.
  • The Estate: 500 acres of world-class training turf.
  • Legacy: A community dedicated to the history of the sport.
